# H5-K2'-1: mixture samplers, surface share, K2' chain

## Mixture samplers (`h5/data.py`)

- **Targets:** `MIX_TARGETS = ("points_mix_a", "points_mix_b")`, built by `build_rayint_item` like the rayint targets
  (`RAYINT_BUILDER_TARGETS = RAYINT_TARGETS + MIX_TARGETS`; `RAYINT_TARGETS` is unchanged). Configs
  `h5/configs/points_mix_{a,b}.yaml`; train, models (`POINT_TARGET_NAMES`), `PointShapeDataset` and
  `scripts/eval_rays.py` accept them.
- **One item, one rng stream:**
  1. the encoder input, as before;
  2. `n_query // 2` near-surface points with `surface_half`, the function that `points_strong` now also uses
     (the same file `points_surface.npz`, both sigma halves, the stored occupancies, one
     `rng.choice(P, n, replace=False)`);
  3. the rays, segments and `t` of the ray-interval sampler for the other `n_query - n_query // 2` points, variant
     a (equal per segment) for points_mix_a and b (proportional to length) for points_mix_b.

  The surface points come first in the arrays, with `from_surface` True.
- **Existing targets unchanged bit for bit:** `surface_half` makes the same draw at the same point of points_strong's
  stream, and the rayint targets draw nothing extra. The stored digests of test_rayint (points_std, points_strong,
  rayint a, b) pass. The one change to test_rayint is the agreement test, which now includes the mixtures:
  `POINT_TARGET_NAMES == POINT_TARGETS + RAYINT_TARGETS + MIX_TARGETS`.
- **Tests (`h5/tests/test_mix.py`, 10):**
  - shapes, dtypes and the two halves;
  - the surface half equals `surface_half` from an rng in the same state, and points_strong's surface half is
    `surface_half` after its uniform half;
  - the ray half has 0 disagreements with `eval_points`;
  - variant a has more than 5 times as many short-segment points as b;
  - determinism;
  - the wiring.

## Surface share (`h5/scripts/k2_surface_share.py`, `h5/results/k2p/sampling_share.{md,json}`)

The items are those of `k2_thin_share.py` (100 train shapes x 20 draws).

**Surface distance**, the same definition for every sampler: per axis, the distance along the axis to the nearest GT
hit on the axis ray through the point's cell; then the minimum over the three axes.

**Thin share** for the mixtures: as in `k2_thin_share`; the surface half is measured as for points_strong, the ray
half along its own ray.

| target | surface distance < 0.002 | surface distance < h/2 | segment < h | segment < 4 h | items |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| points_strong | 0.2236 | 0.3267 | n/a | n/a | 2000 |
| points_rayint_a | 0.0814 | 0.1239 | n/a | n/a | 2000 |
| points_rayint_b | 0.0135 | 0.0255 | n/a | n/a | 2000 |
| points_rayint_c | 0.2631 | 0.3389 | n/a | n/a | 2000 |
| points_mix_a | 0.2600 | 0.3813 | 0.0389 | 0.1304 | 2000 |
| points_mix_b | 0.2259 | 0.3320 | 0.0223 | 0.0840 | 2000 |

**Consistency check (derived, not measured separately):** from mix_b, the near-surface half alone has a
segment-below-h share of about 2 x 0.0223 - 0.0006 = 0.044, taking 0.0006 from rayint_b. Together with rayint_a's
0.0334 this predicts (0.044 + 0.0334) / 2 = 0.039 for mix_a, and 0.0389 is measured.

## Chain (`h5/scripts/k2p.sh`)

- **Root:** `~/PhD/runs/k2p`.
- **Training:** K2p_points_mix_a, then K2p_points_mix_b, with the settings of Q0 and K2: train_subset 32 seed 0,
  eval_on_train, batch 32, 60000 iterations, lr 1e-5 from 50000, validation every 5000.
- **Evaluation:** every checkpoint on the same 32 shapes, all rays, iso grid with the band selection.
- **Report:** `k2_report.py --experiment k2p`, with the K2 runs and Q0_points_strong (`~/PhD/runs/k2`,
  `~/PhD/runs/h5_q0`, read only) side by side at 60000.

## Report (`h5/scripts/k2_report.py`, generalised)

- **Experiments:** `EXPERIMENTS` describes k2 and k2p: prefix, variants, candidates, how the control is used, the
  date the rule was fixed and the title. `build`, `rule` and the markdown are shared.
- **K2 unchanged:** with the K2 arguments the script writes a `report.json` and `report.md` byte-identical to
  `h5/results/k2`.
- **Rule k2p (fixed on 27. 11. 2026):** CONTINUE if K2p_points_mix_a at 60000 has ordinary recall >= 0.702 AND weighted
  thin recall L/h < 1 >= 0.297 AND decoded/GT in band; otherwise STOP.
- **Control:** K2p_points_mix_b is descriptive; the report prints M(a) - M(b) and the weighted thin-recall difference.
