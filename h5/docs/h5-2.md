# h5 step H5-2: ray trunk, heads and losses

Models and losses for the ray decoders, no training loop. Branch `h5`. Nothing in `src/`, `phd_work/`,
`csgbox` or `dexelize` was changed. ConvONet's modules are used unchanged: `LocalPoolPointnet` (encoder)
and `LocalDecoder.sample_plane_feature` (plane sampling, with ConvONet's padding 0.1 and bilinear mode).
No dependency was added.

## Interfaces

### `h5/models.py`

- `ray_sample_points(ray_axis, ray_origin_uv) -> (B, R, 64, 3)` float32. Sample `m` is at axis coordinate
  `BOX_LO + (2m + 1) h`, the centre of cells `2m` and `2m+1`. The two other coordinates are
  `ray_origin_uv` in (col, row) order: (y, z) for x-rays, (x, z) for y-rays, (x, y) for z-rays.
- `summed_plane_feature(p, planes) -> (B, T, 32)`: the sum over `xz`, `xy`, `yz` of
  `LocalDecoder.sample_plane_feature`, in the order of `LocalDecoder.forward`.
- `RayTrunk(c_dim=32, hidden=32, dilations=(1, 2, 4, 8, 16))`: input `fc_p(p) + fc_c(c)` on the
  flattened `(R * 64)` points, then 5 residual blocks along the ray. A block is
  `x + Conv1x1(ReLU(Conv_k3_dil_d(ReLU(x))))` with the last conv zero-initialised. Output `(B, R, 32, 64)`.
- `DenseHead`: `Conv1d(32, 2, 1)`. Output `(B, R, 128)`; cell `2m + i` is channel `i` of sample `m`.
- `HitsHead(K)`: attention `a = Conv1d(32, 4K, 1)` and residual `r = Conv1d(32, 4K, 1)`. Softmax over the
  64 samples, then `s = sum_m p (2m + 1) + sum_m p 2 tanh(r)`, computed in float32 with autocast
  disabled. Mask logits: `Linear(64, 64) -> ReLU -> Linear(64, 2K)` on `[max, mean]` of the trunk features.
  `s` has the t order and the mask logits the mask order of `h5/slots.py`.
- `PlaneHitsHead(K)` (`hits_8_plane`): no trunk. `MLP(32 -> 128 -> 128 -> 6K)`, with
  `s = 64 (tanh(o) + 1)` for the first `4K` outputs and the last `2K` as mask logits.
- `H5RayModel(target, cfg=None)`: `encoder` (ConvONet `LocalPoolPointnet`, `hidden_dim` 32, three
  planes at 64², U-Net `depth 4, start_filts 32, concat`, as in `shapenet_3plane.yaml`), `trunk`
  (`None` for `hits_8_plane`), `head`. `forward(inputs, ray_axis, ray_origin_uv)` returns
  `{"s_pred", "mask_logits"}` for hits targets and `{"logits"}` for dense targets.
  `inputs` is `(B, N, 3)` float32, `ray_axis` `(B, R)` int64, `ray_origin_uv` `(B, R, 2)` float32.
  `n_params(module)` gives the parameter count of a part.

### `h5/losses.py`

- `hits_loss(s_pred, mask_logits, s_tgt, mask_tgt, pos_weight, lam=1.0) -> {total, L_t, L_mask}`.
  Leading dimensions are flattened. Hit slot `h` uses mask `h // 2`. `L_t` is `|s_pred - s_tgt|` in cell
  units, averaged per hit slot over the rays where the slot exists, then averaged over the slots that
  have at least one such ray. `L_mask` is BCE with logits with per-slot `pos_weight` clamped to
  `POS_WEIGHT_CAP = 10`, averaged over rays and slots. `POS_WEIGHT_K2 = (0.4491, 4.0451, 4.0451, 0.4491)`.
- `dense_loss(logits, y, informative, target) -> {total, informative, other}`. `occfrac_128` and
  `binary_128`: BCE with logits. `tsdf_128`: `|2.5 h tanh(logits) - y| / h`. Total
  `0.5 * mean_inf + 0.5 * mean_other`.

## Choices (not obvious from the spec)

1. **Perpendicular plane.** Each ray uses its own plane, and the batch mixes axes. The three planes are
   sampled at all rays and the perpendicular one is selected per ray. The other two samples are
   discarded. This is three grid samples of `R` points, which is negligible next to the trunk. Test c
   checks that changing one plane changes only the rays of that axis.
2. **Ray centre** for `hits_8_plane` is the point on the ray at axis coordinate 0 (box centre).
3. **Empty set in `dense_loss`.** A mean over an empty set is skipped and its 0.5 term is 0. The total
   is not renormalised. So a batch with no informative cells has half the usual loss scale. The spec
   reads this way. If renormalising is wanted, change `dense_loss` and this entry.
4. **`hits_loss` `L_mask` reduction** is a mean over rays and slots (not a sum).
5. **Autocast.** `s_pred` is float32 (elementwise ops inside `autocast(enabled=False)`). The head's conv
   and MLP outputs stay in the autocast dtype. Timing and memory in test g are fp32, with no autocast.
6. **Sampler call.** `LocalDecoder.sample_plane_feature` is called unbound with a namespace holding
   `padding` and `sample_mode`. No decoder weights are created.

### Micro-overfit (test f)

| target | initial loss | loss after 300 steps | drop | needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hits_8 | 25.17 | 7.55 | 70.0 % | > 50 % |
| occfrac_128 | 0.7152 | 0.4298 | 39.9 % | > 50 % |

Values from the last full run. Repeated runs differ in the second decimal place (GPU non-determinism).

**Why occfrac_128 misses 50 %.** The soft BCE on the informative cells has an entropy floor. On this batch
the mean entropy of the informative targets is 0.4999 (`y` averages 0.507 there, 4210 of 262 144 cells).
So the total cannot fall below `0.5 × 0.4999 = 0.250`, which is at most a 65 % drop. A 50 % drop needs a total
below 0.357. A diagnostic run (not committed; same batch, same settings, longer) gave:

| steps | total | informative term | other term |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.715 | 0.697 | 0.733 |
| 300 | 0.429 | 0.664 | 0.193 |
| 1000 | 0.343 | 0.537 | 0.149 |
| 3000 | 0.285 | 0.513 | 0.056 |

So 50 % is reached at about 1000 steps, not 300, and the informative term is close to its floor after 3000.
The criterion as written is not met at 300 steps. I have left the test failing and not changed the threshold
or the step count. Choosing between them is yours.

The peak is probably the trunk activations at `32 × 1024 × 64` samples; this was not profiled.

## Notes and limits

- Test f uses only `hits_8` and `occfrac_128`. `hits_16` and the other dense targets are covered by the shape,
  gradient and loss tests but not by the overfit.
- `hits_loss` takes `pos_weight` as an argument. The K = 4 defaults (cap at 10) are not stored in code.
- The trunk runs at 64 samples per ray for every target. Training memory at larger batches is the open
  question for the training step.
