K2' (memorisation of 32 shapes) passed its rule. K2-3 tests whether the gain generalises.

There is one new run, `K2_3_points_mix_a` (target `points_mix_a`). The points_strong, hits_8, occfrac_128 and
tsdf_128 references come from K1-1 and K1-4; they are not retrained or re-evaluated.

## Chain (`h5/scripts/k2_3.sh`)

The chain is modelled on `k1_1.sh`. Root `~/PhD/runs/k2_3`, log `chain.log`, last line "K2-3 CHAIN DONE".

- **Training:** full train split, batch 32, 120000 iterations, lr 1e-5 from 100000, validation every 10000 with the
  band selection.
- **Evaluation:** checkpoints 40000, 80000 and 120000, evaluated as points_strong was in K1-1: the first 200 val shapes,
  all rays, and the setting chosen by the band selection of training (`q0_report.py eval`, `eval/{run}_it{N}.json`).
- **Report:** `k1_1_report.py --k2-3 ROOT --root ~/PhD/runs/k1_1 --root ~/PhD/runs/k1_4` writes
  `h5/results/k2_3/report.{md,json}`.

## Report (`k1_1_report.py --k2-3`, a flag on the existing script)

- **Unchanged without the flag:** the K1 report is reproduced byte-identical.
- **Rows:** K2_3_points_mix_a at `--new-its`, next to points_strong, hits_8, occfrac_128 (grid, binarize) and tsdf_128
  (grid) at `--its`, all read from their evaluation JSONs.
- **Columns:** ordinary recall 0.002, weighted thin recall L/h < 1 (per hit, population-weighted, as in K2), M,
  precision, decoded/GT, in band, F1 and the ordinary median distance.
- **Per-bin tables:** thin recall at 0.002 and at h/2 at the last checkpoint.
- **Rule (fixed on 27. 11. 2026, at 120000):** CONTINUE if K2_3_points_mix_a has ordinary recall >= 0.271 AND
  M >= 0.031 AND decoded/GT in band; otherwise STOP. The report prints the margin to each threshold, and to the nearer
  band edge.
- **Check line:** the points_strong reference at 120000 must equal `h5/results/k1/report.json` and round to 0.291 / 0.003.
  It does: ordinary recall 0.2911, M 0.002911.

In the dry run, `--new-its` is 10,20,30 and the references stay at 40000/80000/120000, so the reference rows are
filled.
