# H5-K1-1a: band selection of the decoding setting and the K1-1 chain

Status: code and dry runs done. The real chain (`h5/scripts/k1_1.sh`) is **not started**.

## What changed

- `h5/train.py`: `_select` now chooses the decoding setting in two steps (`RATIO_BAND` and `dec_gt_ratio` from
  `eval_rays`). Among the settings whose decoded/GT ratio is in [0.8, 1.25], the best F1 at delta 0.002. If none is in
  the band, the setting whose ratio is closest to the band (distance to the nearer edge; ties go to the higher F1).
  Every validation records `selected_ratio` and `selected_in_band` in `val.jsonl`, and `ratio` and `in_band` in
  `model_it{N}.json` and `model_it{N}.pt`. The grid summaries carry `dec_hits` and `gt_hits`, so no stop was needed.
- `h5/tests/test_select_band.py`: synthetic summaries. (a) best F1 out of band, second best in band, gives the second;
  (b) no setting in band, gives the closest ratio; (c) all in band, gives the plain best F1. Also: distance ties go to
  the higher F1, the lower edge is measured from its own edge, the band edges count as inside, equal F1 goes to the
  first setting, NaN F1 never wins in the band, NaN ratio is never in the band.
- `h5/scripts/q0_report.py eval`: new options `--runs`, `--split`, `--n-shapes`. Without `--split` the behaviour is
  unchanged (training subset of the Q0 runs). With `--split val --n-shapes 200` it evaluates the first 200 validation
  shapes with all rays. Existing `eval/*.json` files are kept.
- `h5/scripts/k1_1_report.py` (new): writes `report.md` and `report.json` from the eval files. Per run and checkpoint:
  ordinary recall at 0.002 and h/2, ordinary median and p90, thin-bin recall per L/h bin, weighted recall of the L/h < 1
  bins (the `M` of H5-6), F1 of all hits at 0.002, precision, decoded/GT, whether that ratio is in the band, and the
  selected setting.
- `h5/scripts/k1_1.sh` (new): hits_8, occfrac_128, points_strong in that order. Full train split, batch 32, 120000
  iterations, learning-rate drop to 1e-5 at 100000, validation every 10000 (200 val shapes, 4096 fixed rays). Then the
  checkpoints 40000, 80000 and 120000 of every run are evaluated on the first 200 val shapes with all rays, and the
  report is written to `h5/results/k1_1`. Resume-safe, `set -euo pipefail`, log in `ROOT/chain.log`. The root is
  `~/PhD/runs/k1_1`.
