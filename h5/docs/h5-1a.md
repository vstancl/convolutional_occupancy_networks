# h5 step H5-1a: ray utilities, slot encoding and hit decoders

numpy-only utilities for the ray decoders (no torch yet). Everything runs in the ConvONet venv
(numpy 1.26.4). csgbox and dexio are reached through `h5/_paths.py:add_external_paths()`, which
appends them to `sys.path`. No dependency was added or changed.

Each module assumes `h5/` is on `sys.path`. The tests and scripts put it there.

## Functions

### `h5/rays.py`

- `load_axis_rayhits(npz_path, axis) -> RayHits`: one axis of `dexels_128.npz` through `dexio.read`.
  `t` float32 becomes float64, `enter` uint8 becomes bool, and `normal` float32 becomes float64.
  Ray order is the file's (`r = i_col + N i_row`).
- `subset_rays(gt, rays) -> RayHits`: CSR gather of the rays in `rays`, in the order given (repeats
  allowed). One vectorized gather, no loop over rays.

### `h5/slots.py`

With `K` intervals per side (K = 2 gives hits_8, K = 4 gives hits_16, i.e. 4K hits).
A ray with `n` hits has `k = n // 2` intervals `I_0 .. I_{k-1}`.

- `encode_slots(gt, K) -> (t (R, 4K) float64, mask (R, 2K) bool)`.
  - Front slot `j` holds `I_j` if `j < k`; back slot `j` holds `I_{k-1-j}` if `j < k`. The two sides are
    filled independently, so duplicates are allowed.
  - `t` order: `[F0.enter, F0.exit, ..., F_{K-1}.exit, B_{K-1}.enter, B_{K-1}.exit, ..., B0.exit]`.
  - `mask` order: `[F0 .. F_{K-1}, B_{K-1} .. B_0]`.
  - Missing slots: `t = 0`, `mask = False`.
- `decode_slots(t, mask_prob, K, thr=0.5, merge_iou=0.5) -> RayHits`:
  1. keep an interval if its slot probability is `> thr` and `enter < exit`;
  2. a front and a back interval with 1-D IoU `>= merge_iou` become one interval, the average of
     their endpoints. Matching is greedy by IoU, each interval in at most one pair, and vectorized
     over rays in at most K rounds;
  3. the remaining intervals are unioned per ray. Overlapping and touching intervals merge.

### `h5/to_hits.py`

- `decode_binary_array(occ (R, M) bool) -> RayHits`: the array form of `csgbox.ceilings.decode_binary`.
  A transition between samples `k` and `k+1` is a hit at their midpoint, and `enter` is the
  transition from outside to inside. The constants `BOX_LO` and `BOX_HI` are checked against
  `csgbox.constants` in the tests.

Synthetic cases of `decode_slots` (K = 2, one row per case, `thr = 0.5`):

1. one interval predicted by a front and a back slot, with offsets 1e-4 and 3e-4: merged to the
   average of the endpoints;
2. two disjoint intervals on the front: both kept;
3. front `[0, 1]` and back `[0.8, 2]` (IoU = 0.1 < 0.5): unioned to `[0, 2]`;
4. front intervals with `enter >= exit` (`[0.5, 0.5]` and `[0.6, 0.4]`): dropped;
5. back interval with probability exactly 0.5: dropped (strict `>`); with 0.6: kept;
6. all probabilities at most 0.5: no hits.

## Positive rate under the training sampler, and pos_weight

Sampler: with probability 1/2 a ray uniform over all `3 * 128^2` rays of the shape, with probability
1/2 a ray uniform over the rays with at least 2 hits (all axes pooled). The mixture rate of slot `s` is
`p_s = 0.5 P_all(s) + 0.5 P_ge2(s)`, pooled over 200 train shapes (`csg/train.lst`, seed 0),
3 axes: `rays_all = 9 830 400`, `rays_ge2 = 3 737 615`. `pos_weight_s = (1 - p_s) / p_s`.

Generated by `h5/scripts/pos_weight.py`.

**K = 2 (hits_8)**: slots `[F0, F1, B1, B0]`

| slot | P_all | P_ge2 | p_mix | pos_weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F0 | 0.380210 | 1.000000 | 0.690105 | 0.4491 |
| F1 | 0.109203 | 0.287218 | 0.198211 | 4.0451 |
| B1 | 0.109203 | 0.287218 | 0.198211 | 4.0451 |
| B0 | 0.380210 | 1.000000 | 0.690105 | 0.4491 |

| slot | P_all | P_ge2 | p_mix | pos_weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| F1 | 0.109203 | 0.287218 | 0.198211 | 4.0451 |

Reading: the front and back sides mirror each other by construction. Slots with `j >= k` are empty,
so deeper slots are rarer, and the rates for rays with at least 2 hits are high for F0 and B0 (1.0,
since every such ray has an interval). The pos_weight of the deep slots (about 31 at K = 4) is large;
clipping or a per-slot focal weight may be needed before training. That choice is for the training
step, not made here.

## Notes

- The tests compare decoded slots against `csgbox.ceilings.decode_hits` on dexel data (float32
  `t`). On float64 analytic GT the same test would need the float32 rounding, which `decode_hits`
  applies internally.
- `decode_slots` merges only front-back pairs. Two front intervals that overlap are unioned, not
  merged by their average.
- The IoU merge is a greedy pairing and is not optimal. The synthetic cases cover the cases the rule
  is meant to handle.
